Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · App: Meow Puzzle (com.brkcmlstudio.app3)

This policy explains how the Android game Meow Puzzle (the "App"), developed by BRKCML Studios, handles your data. In short: the App does not collect, share or sell any personal data.

1. Data we collect

The App does not collect personal data of any kind, such as your name, email, location, contacts, identifiers, device ID or advertising ID. No account or sign-in is required.

2. Data stored on your device

To provide the game experience, the following is stored only in your device's local storage and is never sent off your device:

  • Settings (sound, vibration, dark mode, language, gameplay helpers)
  • Game progress (unlocked and completed levels, total score, daily puzzle streak)
  • Leaderboard entries (time, mistakes, score)

This data never reaches us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or use the reset option in Settings.

3. Internet, ads and third parties

  • The App does not request internet permission and works fully offline.
  • It contains no ads, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • Your data is not shared with any third party.

4. Permissions

The App uses only the vibration (VIBRATE) permission, for in-game haptic feedback, which can be turned off in Settings. It does not request camera, microphone, location, storage, contacts or any other permission.

5. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because it does not collect personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children either.

6. Google Play

When you download the App from Google Play, Google's own privacy policy and data practices apply. This is outside the control of BRKCML Studios.

7. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

Since we do not process personal data, we hold no data about you. You may still contact us with any privacy question or request.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
